# Credit Underwriting & Risk Compliance Policy

## 1. Credit Score Thresholds
- **Tier 1 (Low Risk, Score 750+)**: Automatic eligibility for preferred interest rates.
- **Tier 2 (Moderate Risk, Score 650 - 749)**: Standard underwriting criteria apply. Requires Debt-to-Income (DTI) check.
- **Tier 3 (High Risk, Score < 650)**: High default likelihood. Requires senior underwriter sign-off or co-signer.

## 2. Debt-to-Income (DTI) Ratios
- Maximum allowable DTI ratio for unsecured loans is **45%**.
- DTI between **35% - 45%** triggers a mandatory cash-flow reserve evaluation.
- DTI exceeding **45%** results in mandatory adverse action / denial unless collateral is pledged.

## 3. Delinquencies & Derogatory Marks
- Any active severe delinquency (>90 days past due) within the past 24 months results in immediate application flag.
- More than 2 credit inquiries in the past 6 months adds a risk surcharge to the probability calculation.

## 4. Adverse Action Notice Compliance (FCRA)
Under the Fair Credit Reporting Act (FCRA), any adverse decision (rejection or less favorable terms) must explicitly state the top principal reasons for the decision derived directly from scoring models (e.g. high DTI, low credit score, excessive recent inquiries).
